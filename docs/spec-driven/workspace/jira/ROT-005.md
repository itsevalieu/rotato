## Description

As a user, I want small moments of delight — a confetti burst when I finish a project, gentle ambient sounds, a creative weather mood — so the app feels alive and personal, not sterile.

## Acceptance Criteria

1. Given a project moved to Finished Worlds (drag or menu), then confetti fires once.
2. Given the ambient toggle enabled, then the background animates for the active weather mood.
3. Given ambient sound selected and enabled, then audio plays; disabled, audio stops; preference persists on reload.
4. Given a weather mood selected, then the background updates and preference persists.
5. Given the app idle, then "tended whispers" appear occasionally without user action.

## Tech Lead notes

- Confetti: `useConfetti` hook in `src/hooks/useConfetti.ts`
- Ambient sound: `useAmbientSound` hook in `src/hooks/useAmbientSound.ts`
- Weather: `creativeWeather` in GardenState; `SET_WEATHER` action
- "Once" in AC #1: fire on the move event, not on every render

## Tech Lead flag (Pass 2)

> "AC #5 'tended whispers appear occasionally' — not testable as written. What triggers them? Time-based, or event-based?"

**PM response:** "Time-based — random interval between 3-8 minutes while the app is open. Add this to the AC."

**Revised AC #5:** Given the app has been open for 3+ minutes, then an encouraging whisper appears in the UI at a random interval between 3-8 minutes.
